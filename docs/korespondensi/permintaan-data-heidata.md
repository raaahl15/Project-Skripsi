**Subject:** Request for additional raw images – HIV-1 post-entry steps in resting CD4 T cells (heiDATA doi:10.11588/DATA/MUXYRY)

Dear Dr. Ananth,

My name is Rahul Ghulam Hidayatulloh, an undergraduate student in Engineering Physics at Universitas Gadjah Mada,
Indonesia, supervised by Nopriadi, S.T., M.Sc. My thesis investigates whether machine learning can classify the condition
of HIV-1-exposed CD4 T cells from fluorescence images, using nuclear morphology (Lamin B1) and the subcellular localization
of the HIV-1 capsid, with explainable AI (Grad-CAM) to relate the model's decisions to cell structures.

Your dataset on heiDATA, accompanying your *Cell Reports* paper on HIV-1 post-entry steps in resting CD4 T cells, is the
closest match to this question that I could find, and I am very grateful that it is openly available. In a first feasibility
analysis I segmented around 200 nuclei from the 14 published fields. The data are very informative, but with one field per
condition the classifier mainly learns differences between fields rather than between conditions.

I would therefore like to ask whether it would be possible to share additional raw z-stacks from the same experiments,
in particular:
- more fields of view of resting CD4 T cells infected **with and without Vpx**, and **mock**-treated cells;
- ideally with the same staining as in Fig. 1G or Fig. 3B (Lamin B1 and HIV-1 CA; with HIV-1 GFP if available);
- the original files (e.g. TIFF z-stacks) are perfectly fine; no annotation is needed.

The data would be used only for this non-commercial academic thesis. I would cite your paper and dataset in all outputs,
not redistribute the raw data, and I am happy to share my analysis code and results with you. If sharing is possible,
I can provide a cloud upload link (e.g. Google Drive) for convenience.

Thank you very much for considering this request. I would be happy to provide any further information.
